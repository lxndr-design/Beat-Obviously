Beat compact adaptation

Beat retains unmodified FLAC recordings for selected pitch roots and velocity
layers. It replaces the upstream ARIA-specific SFZ mapping with a Beat-native
multisample map containing nine pitch roots and four velocity layers. This is a
derived mapping of Salamander Grand Piano V3, not the complete upstream library.
