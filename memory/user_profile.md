The user prefers ambitious technical choices over conservative ones. When offered a "safe/easy" option vs a "serious/hard" option, they often pick the harder path (e.g., chose JUCE C++ + web UI hybrid over Tauri-only for Beat). They want long-term expandable foundations rather than fastest-to-MVP.

They make architectural decisions decisively after seeing tradeoffs laid out clearly. Recommend with a clear preference and explain *why*, including what they'd give up — they engage with the tradeoffs.

They are comfortable making big scope decisions ("merge two instruments — be creative") and expect implementations to make sensible choices within the spirit of the spec rather than asking about every detail.
